Current season and rank, how this season is going (from logged sessions), top champion this season, and mains. 2. Four tabs, each with one job: Journal every note and session, newest first (opens first) This season the current season's banner and dashboard Player you across every season (player card) History every past season's banner and stats The tabs are radio buttons styled as tabs, so they work without JavaScript. assets/js/tabs.js only adds nice extras: links like .../#player open that tab, and the address bar follows the tab you pick. Shared links to one entry (#e-...) still work, because Journal is the tab that's open when the page loads.
